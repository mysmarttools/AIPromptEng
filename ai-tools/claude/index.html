What is Claude AI?

Claude is an artificial intelligence assistant developed by Anthropic. It can help users with a wide range of tasks, including writing, summarizing information, brainstorming, coding, research and document analysis.

Claude is particularly useful for people who need help creating and improving written content. It can assist with blog posts, emails, reports, ideas, explanations and many other types of content.
