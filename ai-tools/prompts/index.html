AI PROMPTS GUIDE

What Are AI Prompts?

AI prompts are instructions or questions given to an artificial intelligence system to guide the response. A good prompt provides enough context and direction for the AI to understand what you want it to do.

How to Write a Good AI Prompt

A useful prompt usually explains the task, provides relevant context and describes the desired output. You can also specify the tone, audience, format, length or constraints when those details matter.

1 Role

Tell AI who it should act as.

2 Task

Clearly explain what you need.

3 Context

Provide useful background information.

4 Output

Explain the format or style you want.

AI Prompts for Different Tasks

Prompts can be customized for many use cases including content writing, SEO, programming, business planning, marketing, education, productivity, social media and image generation. The same basic prompt structure can often be adapted to different AI assistants.
