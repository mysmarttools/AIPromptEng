What is Jasper AI?

Jasper AI is an artificial intelligence platform focused on helping businesses and marketers create content more efficiently. It can assist with marketing copy, blog content, social media content, advertising copy and other business communications.

The platform is designed with content and marketing workflows in mind, making it useful for teams, marketers, agencies and businesses that regularly produce digital content.

Jasper AI can be useful for marketers, businesses, agencies and content teams that need to produce marketing content efficiently. Its marketing-focused approach makes it different from general-purpose AI assistants.
