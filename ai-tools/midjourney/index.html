What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an artificial intelligence platform focused on generating images from text-based descriptions. Users can describe a scene, character, object or visual concept and use AI to create images based on their prompts.

The platform is popular among digital artists, designers, creators, marketers and people experimenting with generative AI.

Midjourney can be used to explore creative ideas, develop visual concepts, create illustrations and produce distinctive AI-generated artwork.
