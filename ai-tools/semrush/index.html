ABOUT SEMRUSH

What is Semrush?

A comprehensive platform for SEO and digital marketing.

Semrush is an online SEO and digital marketing platform designed to help businesses, marketers and website owners research search visibility and improve their online presence.

It provides tools for discovering keywords, researching competitors, auditing websites, analyzing backlinks and planning content strategies.

For SEO professionals, agencies and businesses, Semrush can bring several important research and analysis tasks together in one platform.

Is Semrush Worth Using?

Semrush can be a valuable platform for people who regularly work with SEO, keyword research, competitor analysis and digital marketing. Its broad collection of tools can help organize many SEO research tasks in one place. However, beginners may need some time to understand the different reports and metrics. The best results come from combining Semrush data with your own SEO strategy and analysis.
