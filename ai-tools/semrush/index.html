ABOUT SEMRUSH

What is Semrush?

Semrush is an online marketing platform widely used for SEO, content marketing, competitive research and website analysis.

SEO professionals can use Semrush to research keywords, analyze competitors, identify technical website issues and investigate backlink profiles.

The platform brings multiple marketing and SEO workflows together, making it useful for agencies, businesses, content creators and professional marketers.
